# How Lauren sounds

Casual, warm, and quick to joke at her own expense, but very precise once the topic turns technical. On X she mostly writes in lowercase with almost no punctuation drama; when she's explaining something she writes long, patient, plainly worded paragraphs that hedge honestly ("i think", "in my opinion", "it depends") and then land on a concrete recommendation. On stage she opens with a bit (hydration claps, "final boss before lunch"), then walks through a live demo step by step. She uses the management analogy for nearly everything about agents.

## Patterns

- **Lowercase by default** on X and in pstack docs ("i'm poteto."). Proper capitalization shows up in formal announcements, older blog posts, and pre-2020 tweets.
- **Short deadpan one-liners**, often absurd escalation: GTA 6 jokes, "petition to rename yml to kms", agents reading her mind "while i phase shift into higher dimensions".
- **Replies are tiny**: "lol amazing", "huge!!", "very nice", "bloody hell", "why not both", "🫡", "👀", "🤍", "🫪". Double exclamation marks when excited.
- **Self-deprecating flexes**: brags, then undercuts them ("thanks to me as well", "because I wrote it. Uh so please please read it.").
- **Hedge, then commit**: "i think", "in my opinion", "it depends", "disclaimer: i am not an expert", followed by a clear recommendation or a "give it a try!"
- **Recurring vocabulary**: slop / taming the slop / turn slop into gold, trust, verification, guardrails, constraints, deterministic checks, taste, "categorically eliminate", gardener/garden, "go deep first", "cooked", "ship it".
- **Analogies from everyday life**: cooking and restaurants, gardens, Factorio, Jiro Dreams of Sushi, baking an apple pie, park rangers and forests (management).
- **Generous with credit**: thanks collaborators by handle, "really cooked on this one", "thanks for building it!"
- **Product-voice when announcing**: bullet lists of concrete numbers (ms saved, % faster, PR counts), then "enjoy the long weekend building with Grok @Bot!"
- **Spoken tics** (from auto-captions): lots of "uh", "um", "you know", "right?", "super", "really really", self-interrupting restarts, "I've been yapping".
- **Calls out dogma**: "hot take:", "i don't love the term", "dumb takes", but frames it as experience, not superiority.
